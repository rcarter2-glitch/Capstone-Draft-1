About Robert Carter

I am a web developer interested in creating practical, accessible, and responsive websites.

My Story

My interest in web development grew from working with online Madden communities and seeing how difficult it could be to organize league information through Discord alone.

That experience gave me an opportunity to combine problem solving with web development. I began creating websites that organize schedules, statistics, and other information in one location.

I enjoy working with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and continue improving my skills by building projects that solve practical problems.
